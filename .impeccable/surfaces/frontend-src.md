# Backstop app (all screens)

Scope: the whole React app shell plus Overview, Check the claims, Handover. Mode: Operate (Overview leans Persuade inside the same world). Backtest page and the Overview GitHub import box are owned by parallel work: they inherit tokens only.

Audience/job: a lead inspects and signs off claims; a newcomer reads and signs for a role-limited handover; judges do both cold, with no walkthrough.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A handover is a consignment. Knowledge leaves with a declared manifest, every line is inspected against its proof, and the newcomer signs for receipt. Refuses the blue card-grid SaaS dashboard and its dark-terminal opposite.

OWN-WORLD: Form-ink shell (deep blue-green ink header and footer), cool paper ground, white form panels with 1px ink hairlines and 2px corners. Boxed fields: tiny caps label tucked in the box's top-left, value below. Carbon-copy state colours: canary for under inspection and quote highlight, form green approved, carbon pink conflicting/rejected, goldenrod inferred, grey missing. Public Sans for UI, JetBrains Mono for verbatim quotes, line numbers and form numbers, tabular figures.

STORY: Overview says what Backstop is and shows the consignment's live tally; the visitor opens the manifest, inspects a quote highlighted in its source, approves, watches the tally move, switches role on the handover, signs for receipt, prints.

FIRST VIEWPORT (Check the claims): ink bar with brand, consignment number and four numbered step tabs; tally strip of one tick per claim coloured by state; left manifest list (line no., state mark, claim text, kind and type); right inspection form with boxed fields Claim, Evidence, Checks, History; sticky action bar Approve / Edit / Reject with keyboard hints (J/K, A, E, R).

FORM: consignment note / bill of lading, 5 of 7 on the grounded list, seed:5979e065d0a5353b. Signature move: the tally strip, closed by the stamped receipt block on the handover.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
